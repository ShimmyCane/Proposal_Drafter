1. Executive summary

S+Y Studio proposes limited architectural services to support the development of approximately 12 residential units using CRC duplex buildings (three-story, pitched roofs, alley-loaded). Services are scoped to coordinate with the developer (Saral Jalan), produce permit-ready architectural working drawings for plan check, and manage City plan-check coordination. Because the project is early-stage, S+Y recommends a short Discovery to confirm program, planning pathway, and consultant responsibilities prior to committing to a firm fixed fee.

2. Project understanding

Summary of RFQ inputs:

  • Client: Vachi Ventures Development (contact: Saral Jalan)
  • Project: ~12 for-sale residential units assembled as CRC duplex buildings, similar to an attached example (not identical)
  • Scope requested: Architectural project coordination with minimal design input; planning response; architectural working drawings for plan check; City plan check coordination (with Saral or a plan-submittal service)
  • Unknowns: exact unit mix/areas, whether planning review is ministerial or discretionary (SB-1123 implications), who provides structural/MEP/civil/geotech

3. Proposed scope & phases

Phase 0 — Discovery & Assumptions (recommended, fixed-fee)

Objective: quickly confirm program, site/zoning constraints, planning pathway, consultant responsibilities, and deliver a firm fee and schedule.

  • Review example project files, site data, and developer priorities
  • Confirm unit count, typical unit size, parking strategy, setbacks, and potential entitlement triggers
  • Identify required subconsultants and who will contract them
  • Produce a discovery memo, assumptions list, and revised fee & schedule

Phase 1 — Coordination & Limited Design Input

Coordinate with Saral to finalize building massing, unit yield, and critical decisions. Provide minimal schematic adjustments needed to make permit drawings consistent.

Phase 2 — Architectural Working Drawings for Plan Check

Prepare a permit-ready architectural drawing set sufficient for Plan Check:

  • Site plan, floor plans, elevations, roof plan, typical sections
  • Code analysis and plan check submittal checklist
  • Door/window schedules, accessibility notes, and typical construction details

Phase 3 — City Plan Check Coordination

Coordinate or manage the plan-check submittal and responses (options: S+Y or plan-submittal/expediter service per client preference). Include up to two rounds of City comments in base scope; additional rounds are optional services.

Optional Phase 4 — Additional Services (separate fee)

  • Extended design revisions, additional plan-check rounds, construction administration, bidding support, or on-site representation
  • Energy compliance (Title 24), specialty consultants, or discretionary entitlement support (if required)

4. Deliverables & exclusions

Included deliverables:

  • Discovery memo and assumptions
  • Permit-level architectural PDF drawings (and digital CAD/PDF files)
  • Plan-check submittal package and S+Y-prepared response letters (up to 2 rounds)
  • Runbook for plan submittal and coordination notes

Typical exclusions (unless explicitly added):

  • Structural, MEP, civil, geotechnical design (S+Y will coordinate if these are procured by Client)
  • Hazardous materials, environmental reports, or entitlement hearings beyond ministerial reviews
  • Third-party fees (city fees, consultant invoices — billed separately)

5. Budget approach & illustrative fee ranges

Two ways to express a rough budget to Saral:

A) Quick Lump-Sum (recommended for RFQ reply)

Limited (Permit-only + Coordination) — indicative range: $40,000 — $95,000

Notes: Lower range assumes ministerial plan check and timely consultant inputs; upper range assumes discretionary planning, additional plan-check iterations, and complex coordination.

B) Percent of construction cost (if client prefers)

Limited/permit-only services: ~2% — 3.5% of construction cost.

Recommendation: Authorize a short Discovery (e.g., $2,500–$5,000) to firm up assumptions and provide a line-item fee. Discovery reduces contingency and improves estimate accuracy.

6. Schedule & milestones (high-level)

  • Discovery: 1–2 weeks
  • Coordination & concept finalization: 1–2 weeks
  • Permit drawing production: 6–10 weeks
  • Plan-check response & resubmittal: 2–6 weeks

Typical total (permit submittal through first plan-check response): ~10–20 weeks depending on City and consultant turnarounds.

7. Risks & cost drivers

  • Planning pathway: discretionary reviews add significant time and cost
  • Consultant deliverable availability and quality (structural/MEP/civil)
  • City plan-check backlog and comment rounds
  • Site constraints not visible in example project (grading, easements, utilities)
