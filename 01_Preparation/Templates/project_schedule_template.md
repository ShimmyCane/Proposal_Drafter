# S+Y Studio — Master Project Schedule & Timeline Template

This document provides standardized duration baselines, phase dependencies, and agency turnaround assumptions for architectural proposals and client timelines.

---

## 1. Typical Milestone Schedules by Project Archetype

### Archetype 1: Permit-Led Builder's Set (e.g. Duplexes / Small-Lot Subdivision)
*Total Estimated Duration: 14 – 22 Weeks (to Ready to Issue - RTI)*

```
[Contract Signing & Retainer]
  │ (1 - 2 weeks)
  ▼
[Phase I: Concept & Planning Coordination / Civil Map Integration]
  │ (2 - 3 weeks)
  ▼
[Phase II: Site Plan Review / Planning Department Submittal]
  │ (4 - 8 weeks: City Planning Administrative Review)
  ▼
[Planning Approval Received]
  │ (3 - 4 weeks)
  ▼
[Phase III: Construction Documents Preparation & Consultant Integration]
  │ (1 week)
  ▼
[Submit to Building & Safety + County Fire Plan Check]
  │ (4 - 6 weeks: First Round Plan Check by City & Fire)
  ▼
[Receive Plan Check Corrections]
  │ (2 - 3 weeks: Architect & Consultant Team Backcheck Revisions)
  ▼
[Re-submit Backcheck Drawings to City & Fire]
  │ (2 - 3 weeks: Agency Verification & Clearance)
  ▼
[Ready to Issue (RTI) Status Achieved]
```

## 2. Standard Schedule Contingency Clauses for Proposals

When inserting schedules into client proposals, include these protective assumptions:

1. **Client Feedback Windows**: Phase timelines assume client review feedback is returned within 5 to 7 business days of deliverable submission.
2. **Consultant Synchronization**: Timeline depends upon timely receipt of structural, MEP, and civil engineering drawings.
3. **Municipal Plan Check Durations**: Agency review turnaround times (Building & Safety, Fire, Health, Planning) are governed by city staffing and are beyond the Architect's direct control.
