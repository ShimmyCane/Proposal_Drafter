3. Scope of Work

Full Scope Option

Phase I – Pre-Design and Concept Development: We will review the site and project goals, prepare initial design studies, and establish the framework for the project.

Phase II – Design Development: We will develop the design, coordinate with the client and consultants as needed, and refine the project approach.

Phase III – Construction Documentation: We will prepare permit-ready drawings and documentation suitable for submission to the relevant authority.

Phase IV – Permit Support and Coordination: We will assist with coordination, submissions, and responses to plan review comments as required.

Design Only Option

We will provide design services limited to concept development, design development, and preliminary construction documentation for the project.

Permit submission, plan check response, and construction administration are excluded unless separately requested.

Permitting Support Option

We will prepare and revise plan check documents necessary to support permit readiness and assist with the path to RTI status.

This scope assumes that the design intent and prior drawings are available for review and adaptation.
