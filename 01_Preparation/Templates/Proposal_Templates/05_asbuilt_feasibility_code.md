### 2. Scope of Services

#### Phase I — On-Site Field Measurement & Laser Verification
- Conduct comprehensive on-site physical measurement of the existing building using precision laser measuring tools.
- Document interior partition locations, ceiling heights, door/window openings, plumbing fixture locations, and electrical panel points of connection.
- Photograph existing interior and exterior conditions, visible structural framing, and site access.

#### Phase II — As-Built CAD/BIM Base Drawing Production
- Draft measured field data into layered AutoCAD / Revit architectural drawings, including:
  - Existing Site / Plot Plan
  - Existing Dimensioned Floor Plans
  - Existing Exterior Elevations
  - Existing Cross-Sections and Roof Geometry

#### Phase III — Building & Zoning Code Feasibility Report
- Review local zoning ordinance: maximum allowable Floor Area Ratio (FAR), lot coverage, height restrictions, and setback requirements.
- Review California Building Code (CBC) occupancy group classification, construction type, egress compliance, and parking requirements.
- Prepare a concise **Architectural Feasibility Summary Memo** detailing allowable development potential, potential regulatory hurdles, and recommended permitting pathways.

---

### 3. Exclusions & Clarifications

1. **Destructive Testing**: Field measurements reflect visible, accessible existing conditions only. Destructive testing or hidden wall/structural framing investigation is excluded.
2. **Stamped Engineering**: Structural calculations and MEP stamped engineering drawings are excluded.
3. **Subsequent Design/Permit Sets**: Future architectural design, construction documents, and city plan check submittals will be proposed under a separate agreement.
