### 2. Scope of Work & Phased Services

#### Phase I — Concept & Planning Coordination
- Review site zoning, setbacks, lot split geometry, and local building parameters.
- Review client-provided unit test fits and site layout concepts [test fits provided by others].
- Coordinate with Owner’s Civil Engineer for tentative parcel / tract map integration.

#### Phase II — Planning / Site Plan Review Submittal
- Coordinate with Owner’s consultants, including [Civil, Landscape, and Structural].
- Prepare architectural drawings for [City Planning / Development Services] [Tentative Vesting Map / Site Plan Review], including:
  - Architectural Site Plan
  - Architectural Floor Plans (typical and mirrored units)
  - Architectural Exterior Elevations, finishes, and building heights
- Address required planning revisions to obtain Planning approval / clearance [ministerial / administrative].

#### Phase III — Construction Documents & Agency Plan Check
- Create a simplified Construction Drawing Set and submit to [City Building & Safety] and [County Fire Department] Plan Checks, including:
  - Architectural Site Plan, Floor Plans, Roof Plan, Elevations, Building Sections, and Assembly Details.
  - Door and Window Schedules, Finish Legends, and General Notes.
  - Title 24 energy calculations coordination with Owner's MEP engineer.
  - Integration and cross-coordination of Owner’s Civil, Structural, Landscape, and MEP drawing sets.
- Coordinate with agency plan checkers to address correction notices.
- Revise architectural drawings through correction cycles to achieve **Ready to Issue (RTI)** permit status.

### 3. Clarifications & Exclusions

1. **Entitlement Assumptions**: Additional fees may apply if project entitlement is non-ministerial, requires CEQA environmental review, or necessitates public commission hearings.
2. **Consultant Disciplines**: Professional engineering services—including Civil, Landscape, Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP)—are excluded from this architectural scope and shall be contracted directly by the Owner.
3. **Specialty Reports**: Owner is responsible for engaging specialty consultants for Topographic Survey, Geotechnical / Soils Report, Hydrant Flow Test, and related agency-mandated studies.
4. **Fire Sprinkler Systems**: Fire sprinkler design is design-build and considered a **Deferred Submittal**.
5. **Construction Administration**: Full construction observation and site administration are excluded. CA or field reviews may be provided on an hourly basis upon written agreement.
6. **Reimbursable Expenses**: Physical plotting, printing, courier services, and municipal plan check/permit fees are the responsibility of the Owner.
7. **Additional Services**: Scopes of work outside the defined phases will be billed at the standard hourly rate of **[$150 / $175] per hour** upon mutual written agreement.
