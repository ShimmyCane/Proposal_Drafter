### 2. Scope of Services

#### Phase I — Zoning Due Diligence & Preliminary Feasibility
- Research municipal zoning ordinance, specific plans, overlay districts, setbacks, FAR, open space, and parking minimums/maximums.
- Evaluate eligibility under California state housing legislation (SB 684, SB 1123, SB 9, Density Bonus AB 2011, etc.).
- Prepare [1 / 2] conceptual site plan test fits showing building footprints, vehicular circulation, drive aisles, and utility easements.

#### Phase II — Planning Application Package & Agency Submittal
- Prepare architectural entitlement exhibits required for city planning submission:
  - Entitlement Site Plan (dimensions, setbacks, parking matrix, lot coverage).
  - Preliminary Floor Plans & Unit Matrices.
  - Preliminary Exterior Elevations, Maximum Building Height Calculations, and Material Boards.
  - Conceptual 3D Massing Views for Design Review / Planning Staff review.
- Coordinate with Owner’s Civil Engineer regarding Tentative Tract Map / Parcel Map submittal.
