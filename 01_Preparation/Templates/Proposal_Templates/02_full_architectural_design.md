### 2. Scope of Work & Phased Services

#### Phase I — Schematic Design (SD)
- Conduct detailed site evaluation, zoning code analysis, setback review, and allowable FAR/height calculations.
- Develop [2 / 3] distinct conceptual massing, floor plan, and exterior aesthetic options.
- Prepare 3D digital massing visualizations to clarify spatial layout and daylighting.
- Include up to [2] rounds of client design revisions to finalize the preferred schematic design scheme.

#### Phase II — Design Development (DD)
- Refine schematic drawings into dimensioned floor plans, exterior elevations, building sections, and roof geometry.
- Select primary exterior building materials, window/door systems, and structural framing strategies.
- Coordinate with Owner’s Structural, MEP, and Civil engineering consultants to align spatial requirements.
- Produce an integrated design package for client sign-off before commencing technical construction documents.

#### Phase III — Construction Documents (CD) & Permitting
- Prepare complete architectural construction drawing packages for municipal submission, including:
  - Architectural Site Plan, Accessibility/Path of Travel, Life Safety & Code Sheet.
  - Dimensioned Floor Plans, Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP), Roof Plan, and Sections.
  - Exterior Elevations, Typical Wall Assemblies, Waterproofing & Flashing Details.
  - Door, Window, and Interior Finish Schedules.
  - Title 24 energy calculation coordination.
- Submit drawings to [City Building & Safety Department] and coordinate plan check submittals.
- Address city plan check comments across [up to 2] correction cycles to achieve **Ready to Issue (RTI)** status.

#### Phase IV — Bidding Assistance & Construction Administration (CA) [Included / Optional]
- Assist Owner in answering general contractor bidding RFIs (Requests for Information).
- Conduct [X] periodic site observation visits during construction and issue written field reports.
- Review contractor shop drawing submittals and product data for design intent compliance.

### 3. Clarifications & Exclusions

1. **Revisions**: Scope includes up to two (2) consolidated revision cycles per phase. Additional design iterations or significant client program changes after phase sign-off will be billed hourly.
2. **Consultant Engineering**: Structural, MEP, Civil, Landscape, and Geotechnical engineering services are excluded from base architectural fees and shall be contracted directly by Owner (or billed as a pass-through with markup).
3. **Specialty Consultants**: Surveying, environmental reviews (CEQA), arborists, acoustical consulting, and interior decorator services are excluded.
4. **Permit & Plan Check Fees**: All city application, plan check, permit, and utility fees are paid directly by the Owner.
5. **Additional Services**: Out-of-scope services will be billed at the studio standard rate of **[$150 / $175] per hour**.
