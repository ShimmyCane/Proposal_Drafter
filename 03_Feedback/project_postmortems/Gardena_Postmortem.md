# Project Post-Mortem & Proposal Comparison — Gardena Duplexes

* **Project**: Gardena Small-Lot Subdivision (6 Parcels / 12 Units / SB 684 & SB 1123)
* **Client**: Saral Jalan, Vachi Ventures Development LLC
* **Date**: August 2026
* **Comparison**: AI Initial Draft vs. Actual Human Proposal

---

## 1. Context & Client Request
The client sought limited architectural services to produce a builder's permit set for 6 parcels, each featuring a 3-story duplex (~2,500 SF per duplex) with 2-car garages. Units were mirrored/repeated. Floor plan concepts and structural/MEP design were driven by developer parameters; the architect's primary role was site/zoning review, planning submittal for Tentative Vesting Map / Site Plan Review, and construction drawings for Building & Safety / LA County Fire plan check through to Ready to Issue (RTI).

## 3. Durable Takeaways for Future Proposals

1. **Always ask if plans are mirrored/repeated**: Repetitive units reduce drawing production time significantly and allow competitive, profitable fixed-fee pricing.
2. **Clarify the planning pathway upfront**: If the project qualifies for ministerial approval under California housing laws (SB 684, SB 1123, SB 9), distinguish this from discretionary review.
3. **Lock down consultant boundaries**: Make it crystal clear that the owner hires Civil, Structural, MEP, Soils, and Surveyor directly, and that fire sprinklers are a deferred design-build submittal.
