# S+Y Studio — Lessons Learned & Studio Insights

This document is the human-readable repository of studio insights, fee benchmarks, scope boundaries, and quoting lessons derived from real project outcomes and proposal reviews.

---

## 1. Core Scoping & Quoting Rules

### A. The "Prompt-First" Scoping Rule
* **Rule**: Never draft a proposal assuming a full architectural design commission by default.
* **Why**: Many developer and builder commissions (e.g., Gardena duplexes) are **permit-led / builder's sets** where floor plans are already mirrored or fixed, finishes are owner-selected, and the architect's true scope is zoning review, planning submittals, construction drawings, and agency plan check coordination.
* **Impact**: Over-scoping a proposal with unnecessary "design discovery" alienates developer clients and inflates the perceived fee.

### B. Milestone Payment Structure vs. Hourly Ranges
* **Rule**: Fixed fees tied to clear, verifiable project milestones win client trust and maintain studio cash flow.
* **Standard S+Y Milestones**:
  1. **Retainer / Deposit**: 5% – 15% (upon contract execution)
  2. **Planning / Site Plan Review Submittal**: 20% – 25% (deliverable submission)
  3. **Building & Safety Plan Check Submittal**: 40% – 50% (permit set completion)
  4. **Ready to Issue (RTI) / Clearance**: 20% – 25% (correction rounds completed)
* **Hourly Add-on Rate**: $150 – $175 / hour for out-of-scope services or extended review beyond agreed revision cycles.

### C. Standard Inclusions vs. Non-Negotiable Exclusions
* **Always Explicitly Exclude (unless contracted separately)**:
  - Engineering disciplines: Structural, MEP, Civil, Landscape, Traffic.
  - Site investigation: Topographic survey, Geotechnical / soils report, Fire hydrant flow test.
  - Specialty systems: Fire sprinkler design (must be designated as **Deferred Submittal / Design-Build**).
  - Construction Administration: Standard proposals exclude full CA (available as additional hourly service).
  - Plotting & reproduction expenses: Reimbursable at cost.
  - Discretionary or public entitlement hearings (unless scoping an entitlement package).

## 2. Benchmark Fee & Hourly Estimates

| Project Archetype | Scope Indicator | Benchmark Pricing Logic | Typical Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Permit-Led Builder's Set** | Small-lot subdivision, SB 684/1123, mirrored units | $5,000 – $6,000 / unit or fixed milestone bundle ($50k–$75k for 6–12 units) | 8–16 weeks to RTI |
| **02. Full Architectural Design** | Custom residential, new build, extensive concept | 10%–15% of construction cost, or $150–$175/hr task sum ($30k–$80k+) | 16–32 weeks |
| **03. Tenant Improvement (TI)** | Commercial / Retail / Residential Remodel | $4.50 – $7.50 / SF or fixed ($12k–$25k) | 6–12 weeks |
| **04. Planning & Entitlement** | Vesting tract maps, site plan review, variances | Fixed phase fee ($10k–$20k) | 8–20 weeks (agency dependent) |
| **05. As-Built & Feasibility** | Field verification, CAD/BIM model, code review | $1.50 – $2.50 / SF or fixed ($3k–$8k) | 1–3 weeks |
