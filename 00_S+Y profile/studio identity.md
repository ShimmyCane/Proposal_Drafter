# S+Y Studio Identity

## Studio approach

S+Y Studio approaches projects in a collaborative, transparent, and thoughtful way. The studio aims to be clear up front, set expectations early, and use modeling and visual communication to help clients understand design direction.

## Core principles

- Collaborative working style
- Clear communication from the start
- Use of modeling and visual tools to support design clarity
- Strong upfront scope definition
- Professional, persuasive, and confident proposal tone
- Flexible enough to serve many project types while remaining structured

## Proposal philosophy

The studio generally works on a fixed-fee basis, while still checking project hours against the project schedule and fee schedule to ensure the scope remains realistic and sustainable. The studio also anticipates client needs and tries to build in helpful support without making the proposal feel overly restrictive.

## Revision approach

Two rounds of revisions are considered a fair and practical standard. This helps maintain momentum while keeping the process manageable.

## Additional services approach

Rather than making clients feel constrained by a long list of exclusions, the studio prefers to maintain a broad library of additional services that can be pulled in as needed. This keeps the proposal welcoming while still protecting scope and allowing for flexibility.

## Proposal framework posture

The studio should use a comprehensive proposal framework that can capture the full range of project types, while also supporting separate templates for specific project categories such as:

- Residential
- Tenant improvement
- Permitting support
- New build or mixed-use projects

## Tone and presentation

Proposals should feel professional, persuasive, and clear. The tone should be confident and client-centered without sounding overly rigid or intimidating.

## Notes for future refinement

This document should continue to evolve as the studio refines its process, pricing logic, and proposal language. It can be used to guide future proposal drafts and to keep the studio voice consistent.
