# Skill: `refine`

## Purpose
Focus targeted edits on a single selected document within the context of a project without modifying the rest of the workspace.

---

## Inputs
- `targetFilePath` (string; path to the document to refine)
- `instructions` (string; specific edits or refinements requested by user)

---

## Outputs
- Targeted edits applied directly to the document.
- Brief summary of changes made.
